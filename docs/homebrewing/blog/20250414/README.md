# Bottling day @ April 14th, 2025

This morning I bottled the 3 variants of my English porter brew.

Scores on the door:

OG was 1.052 for all three batches, temperature controlled fridge, sensor on
the bucket with batch #44.

Batch #44 with Fermentis S04 English ale yeast ended with a FG of 1.010 -->
giving an Apparent Attenuation (AA) of 81.4%, firm yeast cake.

Batch #45 with Lallemand Windsor yeast ended with a FG of 1.015 --> giving an AA
of 71.8%, fluffy yeast cake.

Batch #46 with Lallemand Nottingham yeast ended with a FG of 1.008 --> giving an
AA of 84.9%, firm yeast cake.
 
First slurp gave the impression if a bit strong on coffee/chocolate, maybe lower
the chocolate malts to 35 grams in a next attempt.

Now I'm impatient to do a side by side comparison in about two weeks 😎
