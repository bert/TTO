# Beer tasting days @ May 9th, 2025

 This week I have been tasting my English porter variants I brewed with varying
 yeasts.
 
 I tasted each of them with a day, or so, in between.
 
 All of them had too much chocolate flavour, blanketing hops and yeast flavours.
 
 Of all the variants, the Lallemand Windsor yeast came out best followed by
 the Lallemand Nottingham yeast, the Fermentis S-04 came last.
 
 It was a close match, no outliers here.
