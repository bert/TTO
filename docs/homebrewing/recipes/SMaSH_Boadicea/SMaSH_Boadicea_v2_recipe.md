# SMaSH Boadicea v2

Style: 12A British Golden Ale

ABV: 3.9 %

IBU: 31

EBC: 8

Original Gravity: 1.039

Final Gravity 1.010

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: NL Spa Reine Flat Mineral Water (www.ah.nl)

Target profile: British Golden Ale - Mean Brews Profile

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 58    | 6     | 17   | 46   | 107    | 32   | ppm |

Strike temperature @ 73.3 C

60 minutes @ 67 C stir frequently

1000 grams Muntons Maris Otter malt

## Boil

60 minutes

3 grams Boadicea (A.A. 8.1 %) @ 0 minutes (13.0 IBU)

4 grams Boadicea (A.A. 8.1 %) @ 30 minutes (13.3 IBU)

1 grams Irish Moss @ 50 minutes

## Hopstand

10 grams Boadicea (A.A. 8.1 %) 15 minutes hopstand @ 80 C (4.5 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.5 package NBS Classic English Ale yeast

Primary @ 18 C for 10 days

Diacetyl rest @ 21 C for 4 days

## Bottling

23 gram Table Sugar for 2.2 % carbonation

Carbonation @ 18 C for 14 days

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 18 C for 28 days
