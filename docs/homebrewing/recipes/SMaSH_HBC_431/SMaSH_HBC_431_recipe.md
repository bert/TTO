# SMaSH Godiva

Style: 18B American Pale Ale

ABV: 5.4 %

IBU: 43

EBC: 11.8

Original Gravity: 1.048

Final Gravity 1.009

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: NL Hoofddorp Rein Tap Water (2020-Q1 WQR)

Target profile: American Pale Ale - Mean Brews

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 109   | 9     | 26   | 75   | 233    | 17   | ppm |

Strike temperature @ 71 C

60 minutes @ 65 C

1200 grams Castle Malting Chateau Pale Ale Malt

## Boil

60 minutes

3 grams HBC 431 (A.A. 15.3 % pellet) @ 30 minutes (17.7 IBU)

5 grams HBC 431 (A.A. 15.3 % pellet) @ 50 minutes (16 IBU)

1 grams Irish Moss @ 50 minutes

## Hopstand

10 grams HBC 431 (A.A. 15.3 % pellet) 20 minutes @ 80 C (9.6 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.5 package Fermentis US-05 SafAle American Yeast

Primary @ 20 C for 14 days

25 grams HBC 431 (A.A. 15.3 % pellet) dry hopping for 6 days

## Bottling

32 gram Table Sugar for 2.5 % carbonation

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 20 C for 45 days
