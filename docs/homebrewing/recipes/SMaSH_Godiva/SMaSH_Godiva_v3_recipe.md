# SMaSH Godiva v3

Style: 12A British Golden Ale

ABV: 4.2 %

IBU: 26

EBC: 7.1

Original Gravity: 1.039

Final Gravity 1.009

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: 02 NL Spa Reine Flat Mineral water (www.ah.nl)

Target profile: Blonde Ale

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 55    | 15    | 45   | 75   | 150    | 40   | ppm |

Mash pH: 5.37

Strike temperature @ 68.7 C

60 minutes @ 63 C

1000 grams Paul's Malt British Breeze Maris Otter Malt

## Boil

60 minutes

5 grams Godiva (A.A. 7.5 % whole) @ 30 minutes (14.4 IBU)

5 grams Godiva (A.A. 7.5 % whole) @ 45 minutes (9.8 IBU)

1 grams Irish Moss @ 50 minutes

## Hopstand

5 grams Godiva (A.A. 7.5 % whole) 15 minutes @ 80 C (1.9 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.5 package Lallemand Nottingham Yeast

Primary @ 17 C for 10 days

7 grams Godiva (A.A. 7.5 % whole) for 7 days

Diacetyl rest @ 21 C for 4 days 

## Bottling

30 gram Table Sugar for 2.3 % carbonation

## Carbonation

Carbonation @ 17 C for 14 days

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 17 C for 28 days
