# SMaSH Progress v1

Style: 12A British Golden Ale

ABV: 4.3 %

IBU: 36

EBC: 9.5

Original Gravity: 1.043

Final Gravity 1.010

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: NL Spa Reine Flat Mineral Water (www.ah.nl)

Target profile: British Golden Ale Profile

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 58    | 6     | 17   | 46   | 107    | 32   | ppm |

Strike temperature @ 71 C

60 minutes @ 65 C stir frequently

1000 grams Brewferm Pale Ale malt

## Boil

60 minutes

7 grams Progress (A.A. 7.6 %) @ 30 minutes (21.4 IBU)

7 grams Progress (A.A. 7.6 %) @ 50 minutes (11.4 IBU)

1 grams Irish Moss @ 50 minutes

## Hopstand

7 grams Progress (A.A. 7.6 %) 15 minutes hopstand @ 80 C (2.9 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.5 package Fermentis S-04 yeast

Primary @ 18 C for 10 days

5.6 grams Progress (A.A. 7.6 %) for 4 days

Diacetyl rest @ 21 C for 4 days

## Bottling

23 gram Table Sugar for 2.2 % carbonation

Carbonation @ 18 C for 14 days

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 18 C for 28 days
