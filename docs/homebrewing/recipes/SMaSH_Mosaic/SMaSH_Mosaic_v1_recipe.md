# SMaSH Mosaic v1

Style: 01D American Wheat Beer

ABV: 4.6 %

IBU: 20

EBC: 7.5

Original Gravity: 1.042

Final Gravity 1.007

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: NL Spa Reine Flat Mineral Water (www.ah.nl)

Target profile: American Wheat/Rye Profile

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 55    | 15    | 46   | 75   | 150    | 40   | ppm |

Strike temperature @ 68.7 C

60 minutes @ 63 C stir frequently

1000 grams Brewferm Wheat malt

## Boil

60 minutes

2 grams Mosaic (A.A. 11.6 %) @ 40 minutes (7.5 IBU)

3 grams Mosaic (A.A. 11.6 %) @ 50 minutes (7.3 IBU)

## Hopstand

10 grams Mosaic (A.A. 11.6 %) 20 minutes hopstand @ 75 C (5.1 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.5 package Fermentis US-05 American yeast

Primary @ 20 C for 10 days

11.2 grams Mosaic (A.A. 11.6 %) for 4 days

Diacetyl rest @ 23 C for 4 days

## Bottling

30 gram Table Sugar for 2.4 % carbonation

Carbonation @ 20 C for 14 days

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 20 C for 28 days
