# SMaSH CF184 v1

Style: 12A British Golden Ale

ABV: 4.3 %

IBU: 29

EBC: 8.1

Original Gravity: 1.043

Final Gravity 1.010

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: NL Spa Reine Flat Mineral Water (www.ah.nl)

Target profile: British Golden Ale - Mean Brews Profile

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 58    | 6     | 17   | 46   | 107    | 32   | ppm |

Strike temperature @ 68.7 C

60 minutes @ 63 C stir frequently

1000 grams Thomas Fawcett Maris Otter Pale Ale malt

## Boil

60 minutes

5 grams CF184 (A.A. 8.4 %) @ 40 minutes (13.4 IBU)

5 grams CF184 (A.A. 8.4 %) @ 50 minutes (8.5 IBU)

1 grams Irish Moss @ 50 minutes

5 grams CF184 (A.A. 8.4 %) @ 55 minutes (5.3 IBU)

## Hopstand

5 grams CF184 (A.A. 8.4 %) 15 minutes hopstand @ 80 C (1.5 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.5 package Lallemand Nottingham yeast

Primary @ 18 C for 10 days

4 grams CF184 (A.A. 8.4 %) for 7 days

Diacetyl rest @ 21 C for 4 days

## Bottling

23 gram Table Sugar for 2.2 % carbonation

Carbonation @ 18 C for 14 days

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 18 C for 28 days
