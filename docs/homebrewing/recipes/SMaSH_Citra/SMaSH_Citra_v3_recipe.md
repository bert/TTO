# SMaSH Citra v3

Style: 18A Blonde Ale

ABV: 4.9 %

IBU: 24

EBC: 11.2

Original Gravity: 1.047

Final Gravity 1.010

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: NL Spa Reine Flat Mineral Water (www.ah.nl)

Target profile: Blonde Ale Profile

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 55    | 15    | 45   | 75   | 150    | 40   | ppm |

Strike temperature @ 71 C

60 minutes @ 65 C

1000 grams Castle Malting Chateau Pale Ale malt

## Boil

60 minutes

1.5 grams Citra (A.A. 13.8 %) @ 30 minutes (8.1 IBU)

1.5 grams Citra (A.A. 13.8 %) @ 45 minutes (5.4 IBU)

1 grams Irish Moss @ 50 minutes

4 grams Citra (A.A. 13.8 %) @ 55 minutes (6.9 IBU)

## Hopstand

6 grams Citra (A.A. 13.8 %) 10 minutes hopstand @ 80 C (3.2 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.3 package Lallemand Voss Kveik yeast

Primary @ 33 C for 7 days

8 grams Citra (A.A. 13.8 %) for 2 days

## Bottling

35.5 gram Table Sugar for 2.4 % carbonation

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 33 C for 14 days
