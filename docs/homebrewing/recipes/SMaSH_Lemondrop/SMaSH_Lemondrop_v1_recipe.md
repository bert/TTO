# SMaSH Lemondrop v1

Style: 18A Blonde Ale

ABV: 4.9 %

IBU: 20

EBC: 7.7

Original Gravity: 1.045

Final Gravity 1.008

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: NL Spa Reine Flat Mineral Water (www.ah.nl)

Target profile: Blonde Ale Profile

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 55    | 15    | 45   | 75   | 150    | 40   | ppm |

Strike temperature @ 68.7 C

60 minutes @ 63 C stir frequently

1100 grams Simpsons Finest Pale Maris Otter malt

## Boil

60 minutes

6 grams Lemondrop (A.A. 5.2 %) @ 30 minutes (12.3 IBU)

1 grams Irish Moss @ 50 minutes

6 grams Lemondrop (A.A. 5.2 %) @ 50 minutes (6.2 IBU)

## Hopstand

8 grams Lemondrop (A.A. 5.2 %) 15 minutes hopstand @ 75 C (1.5 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.5 package Fermentis US-05 American yeast

Primary @ 20 C for 10 days

Diacetyl rest @ 23 C for 4 days

## Bottling

30 gram Table Sugar for 2.4 % carbonation

Carbonation @ 20 C for 14 days

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 20 C for 28 days
