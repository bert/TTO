# SMaSH Magnum v1

Style: 18A Blonde Ale

ABV: 5.1 %

IBU: 22

EBC: 8.7

Original Gravity: 1.047

Final Gravity 1.008

Batch size: 5 L

## Mash

Source profile: NL Spa Reine Flat Mineral Water (www.ah.nl)

Target profile: Blonde Ale Profile

| Ca 2+ | Mg 2+ | Na + | Cl - | SO4 2- | HCO3 |     |
|-------|-------|------|------|--------|------|-----|
| 55    | 15    | 45   | 75   | 150    | 40   | ppm |

Strike temperature @ 68.7 C

60 minutes @ 63 C

1100 grams Thomas Fawcett Maris Otter Pale Ale malt

## Boil

60 minutes

3 grams Magnum (A.A. 10.7 %) @ 30 minutes (12.6 IBU)

3 grams Magnum (A.A. 10.7 %) @ 50 minutes (6.6 IBU)

1 grams Irish Moss @ 50 minutes

## Hopstand

4 grams Magnum (A.A. 10.7 %) 15 minutes hopstand @ 80 C (2.3 IBU)

## Fermentation

0.5 package Fermentis US-05 yeast

Primary @ 17 C for 10 days

Diacetyl rest @ 21 C for 4 days

## Bottling

30 gram Table Sugar for 2.4 % carbonation

## Conditioning

Conditioning @ 17 C for 42 days
